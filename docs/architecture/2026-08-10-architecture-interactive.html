Invariants that do not change

  • PDW = Pool Deck (Sam / AQPN, Supabase) · SDW = Splashworks (Ross / CCE, Postgres). Boundary: SUPABASE_* vs DATABASE_URL.
  • No PDW data is ingested into SDW models — backups are held as verified files; RC-9 reads them for reconciliation only.
  • AQPN org is Lex Immutabilis: local review only; every PDW change is Sam's to make.
  • SDW stays read-only toward Skimmer — no write-back adoption.
